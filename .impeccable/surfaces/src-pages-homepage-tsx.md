## Direction contract

THESIS: The Home reads as an annotated technical drawing set — services, process, and projects presented as architect's plans, not marketing cards. Refuses the gradient-hero-and-blob default every agency site ships.

OWN-WORLD: Deep cyanotype blue (~#0E2F5A) as the dominant structural ground across hero and section dividers; warm vellum/paper (~#F4EDE0) only inside dense-content panels (service detail, project cards); graphite ink for body text; a stamp-red accent (~#B5432E, "REVISADO"/"APROBADO" rubber-stamp marks) for CTAs and status. Headlines in Architects Daughter (hand-drafted architect lettering); body in Archivo; annotations, measurements, and revision numbers in JetBrains Mono. Drafting-table motifs throughout: ruled grid lines, set-square/T-square marks, leader-line callouts, rubber stamps, revision-number tags.

STORY: The visitor understands BApps draws the plan before writing code — confirming the right thing gets built. They read this as a small, direct, technically serious team, not a template shop. They act by requesting a free quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Hero as a full-bleed drawing sheet — architect-lettered headline over the cyanotype ground, leader-lines pointing from measurement-style callouts to the key phrases of the subtitle, a "PRESUPUESTO GRATIS" rubber-stamp as the primary CTA, a faint T-square/grid watermark behind it all.

FORM: Blueprint / technical drafting ("El Plano") — candidate 1 of my own grounded list (assigned was 6, Fileteado Porteño; user pinned this pick over the roll). Seed key 4e9448d1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
